# ChessBuild Academy ♞

Personalized online chess coaching for ages 7–11, helping children build strong fundamentals, confidence, problem-solving abilities, and independent thinking on the board.

**Live Website:** [https://chessbuilt-academy.vercel.app/](https://chessbuilt-academy.vercel.app/)

## About
Founded by Priyanka, a state-level chess player with 8+ years of competitive experience and 3+ years of coaching. Classes are conducted one-to-one via Google Meet in English and Tamil.
